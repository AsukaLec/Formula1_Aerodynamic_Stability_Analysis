## Final Diagnosis Summary

### Verdict: Wing has real but saturated effect — model-driven convergence to lower bound

The surrogate model (XGBoost) **does perceive wing angle**, but the effect manifests only in **non-optimal regions** of the parameter space.

**Evidence**:

| Test | Result | Interpretation |
|------|:---:|------|
| Task A: wing sweep at fixed configs | ΔStability = **0.88 ~ 2.15** across 16 wing values | Model IS sensitive to wing at moderate stability levels (~72-74) |
| Task A: per-speed mean slope | -0.06 to **-0.13** per degree | Higher wing → lower stability (model direction is clear) |
| Task B: speed×wing gradient | max gradient = **0.63** | Wing effect varies with speed — interaction exists |
| Task E: r(wing, stability) at v>260 | r = **-0.66** | Strong negative correlation in high-speed data |
| Task E: partial r (wing\|downforce) | r = **0.06** | After controlling for downforce, wing's independent effect is negligible |

**The key insight**: PSO operates in the **high-stability saturation region** (stability≈100, downforce≈4400N, drag≈18N). In this region, model predictions are near-constant regardless of wing. The model's wing sensitivity (Δ=2.15) only appears when downforce and drag are constrained to moderate values.

This is **Hypothesis B (data-driven) with a nuance**: it's not that the model never sees wing — it sees wing at moderate stability levels, but the optimization landscape flattens at the high-stability plateau that PSO naturally converges to.

**Implication for T06-v3**: The Robust Multi-Condition Optimization remains a valid direction, but MUST constrain the optimization to avoid the saturated region. Specifically:

1. **Introduce efficiency constraints** that force PSO away from drag=18 (the saturation attractor)
2. **Cap stability at 95** in the fitness calculation to keep the model in its sensitive regime
3. **Add a diversity bonus** that rewards solutions with differentiation across speed points

OR accept that the multi-objective optimization's value is in demonstrating **how to build the framework** rather than producing differentiated wing optima from this specific surrogate model.

### Recommendation

Defer T06-v3 implementation. Record the following in T06 task document:

> Landscape Diagnosis (Tasks A-E) found that the surrogate model does encode
> wing→stability relationships (Δ≈2 points at moderate stability, r≈-0.66 at
> high speed), but these relationships are **masked by model saturation** in
> the high-stability region (stability≈100) where PSO inevitably converges.
> Wing→20° is neither an optimizer failure nor a data absence — it is the
> correct optimum of a model whose high-stability plateau has been inflated
> by data imbalance (85% stability≥99).
>
> The multi-objective extension (v2) successfully differentiated scenarios by
> composite fitness (0.685 vs 0.894), but failed to produce wing tradeoffs
