## 1. `model_comparison_r2.png`

- **What**: Grouped bar chart of R2 and R2_low across all 6 models.
- **R2**: coefficient of determination on the full test set (closer to 1 = better).
- **R2_low**: R2 restricted to unstable samples (stability_index < 95).
- **R2_high is excluded** because near-zero variance at stability >= 95 makes R2
  numerically unstable (denominator ~0). Actual MSE_high is extremely low.

### Key values

| Model | R2 | R2_low |
|-------|------|------|
| M1_Ridge | 0.633 | 0.78 |
| M2_RandomForest | 1.000 | 1.000 |
| M3_XGBoost | 0.9999 | 1.000 |
| M4_MLP | 0.9994 | 0.999 |
| M5_TabNet | 0.9997 | 0.9998 |
| M6_DeepEnsemble | 0.9995 | 0.999 |

### Interpretation

- Ridge (0.633) is the performance lower bound, confirming strong nonlinearity.
- All non-linear models achieve R2 >= 0.9994 and R2_low >= 0.999.

- **Why log**: Ridge errors (MSE=225, MAE=12.3) are 500-12000x larger than

|-------|------|------|------|

| M3_XGBoost | 0.031 | 0.035 | 0.177 |
| M4_MLP | 0.390 | 0.388 | 0.624 |
| M5_TabNet | 0.200 | 0.360 | 0.447 |
| M6_DeepEnsemble | 0.327 | 0.271 | 0.572 |

### Interpretation

  but still well within the target threshold (MSE < 0.05 ideal, < 0.02 goal).

## 3. `model_comparison_latency.png`

- **What**: Grouped bar chart of inference latency per sample. **Log scale**.
- **Why log**: RF (37ms) is 150x slower than XGBoost (0.24ms).
  Linear scale would hide the sub-millisecond models entirely.
- **Critical for PSO**: thousands of fitness evaluations per run.

### Key values

| Model | Latency_ms |
|-------|------------|
| M1_Ridge | 0.034 |
| M2_RandomForest | 37.073 |
| M3_XGBoost | 0.242 |
| M4_MLP | 0.403 |
| M5_TabNet | 7.847 |
| M6_DeepEnsemble | 1.230 |

### Interpretation

- **XGBoost at 0.24 ms is the clear PSO inference choice** --
  150x faster than RF with only a 0.01% R2 penalty.
- MLP at 0.40 ms is a viable alternative with uncertainty support (DeepEnsemble).
- TabNet at 7.85 ms is too slow for iterative optimization workflows.
- RF at 37 ms is accurate but impractical for high-frequency PSO calls.

## 5. `predicted_vs_actual.png`

- **What**: 2x3 grid of scatter plots, one per model.
- **X axis**: Actual stability_index (ground truth, 0-100).
- **Y axis**: Predicted stability_index (model output, 0-100).
- **Red dashed line**: y = x -- perfect predictions would lie exactly on this line.
- **Interpretation**:
  - Ridge: wide spread, spindle-shaped cloud -- linear model captures only the broad trend.
  - RF / XGBoost: near-perfect adherence to the diagonal, confirming the near-deterministic
    nature of the dataset.
  - MLP / TabNet / DeepEnsemble: tight to the diagonal, with slight scatter in the
    low-stability region (y < 20) where samples are rarest.
  - RF points form an almost perfect line -- this pattern, combined with the
    zero-overlap check between train and test, confirms the R2=1.000 is
    genuine generalization, not data leakage.

## 6. `predicted_vs_actual_stratified.png`

- **What**: Stratified-sampled scatter of predicted vs actual stability_index.
  500 points are drawn from each stability bin (severe/moderate/mild/stable),
  giving equal visual weight to all regimes instead of being dominated by
  the 86% stable samples.
- **Color coding**:
  - Red: severe [0, 30)
  - Orange: moderate [30, 60)
  - Blue: mild [60, 95)
  - Grey: stable [95, 101)
- **Black dashed line**: y = x.
- **Interpretation**:
  - Orange/red dots (low stability) allow direct inspection of worst-regime
    accuracy. The diagonal spread in these bins reveals real prediction error.
  - All models except Ridge show excellent adherence to the diagonal in all
    bins. Ridge deviates at low stability values.
  - Unlike the full scatter (`predicted_vs_actual.png`), the stratified
    version makes rare unstable samples immediately visible rather than
    drowned by 19,000 overlapping stable points.

## 7. `training_loss_curves.png`

- **What**: Training and validation loss curves for all neural-network models.
- **Left panel (Train Loss)**: MSE on the training set per epoch.
- **Right panel (Val Loss)**: MSE on the validation set per epoch.
- **Legend**:
  - M4_MLP: the single MLP baseline.
  - M6_DeepEnsemble_0 / _1 / _2: the three ensemble members.
- **Interpretation**:
  - All curves descend smoothly without large oscillations -- the learning rate
    (1e-3) and batch size (256) are well-chosen.
  - Validation loss does not rise after convergence -- no overfitting,
    early stopping (patience=20) is effective.
  - The three DeepEnsemble members exhibit slightly different loss trajectories --
    different random seeds (42, 142, 242) produce meaningful model diversity,
    which is essential for reliable uncertainty estimation.

## 8. `uncertainty_distribution.png`

- **What**: Histogram of DeepEnsemble prediction standard deviation sigma across samples.
- **X axis**: Prediction Std (sigma, the standard deviation of 3 ensemble member
  predictions for the same sample).
- **Y axis**: Frequency (number of samples).
- **Blue**: Sigma distribution on 5,000 training samples.
- **Coral / Red**: Sigma distribution on the full test set.
- **Key values**:
  - Mean sigma (train 5k): **0.491**
  - Mean sigma (test): **0.493**
  - Max sigma (test): **4.35** (in the extreme low-stability regime, y < 10)
- **Interpretation**:
  - The sigma distributions for train and test are nearly identical --
    the ensemble does not exhibit larger disagreement on unseen data,
    indicating **no severe out-of-distribution (OOD) problem**.
  - The peak around sigma = 0.3-0.5 reflects high consensus in the
    high-stability region (y ~ 100) where all 3 members agree.
  - The long tail (sigma > 1.0) corresponds to sparse, low-stability
    regions where the 3 members diverge -- **sigma can serve as the
    uncertainty penalty term for risk-sensitive PSO in T04**.

## 9-10. Response Surface Plots (2 x `response_surface_*.png`)

### Common conventions

- **What**: 2D contour heatmaps of predicted stability_index generated by the
  best model (M2_RandomForest) on an 80 x 80 parameter grid.
- **Color scale**: RdYlGn -- red = low stability (unstable), green = high stability.
- **Grid resolution**: 80 x 80 = 6,400 evaluation points per surface.
- **KNN context**: `downforce_n` and `drag_n` are NOT independent --
  they are outputs of the same CFD simulation.  For each grid point,
  the 10 nearest training samples in (speed, wing, DRS) space are found
  and their median downforce/drag values are used.  This ensures every
  query point is physically consistent (no OOD extrapolation).
- **Purpose**: Physical consistency verification -- do the model predictions
  conform to aerodynamic common sense?

---

### 9. `response_surface_speed_kmh_wing_angle_deg_DRS0.png`
### 10. `response_surface_speed_kmh_wing_angle_deg_DRS1.png`

- **X axis**: speed_kmh (100-365 km/h)
- **Y axis**: wing_angle_deg (5-35 degrees)
- **DRS=0**: DRS closed (cornering / braking scenario)
- **DRS=1**: DRS open (straight-line scenario)
- **Numerical features**:
  - Upper-right (high speed + large wing angle): red/yellow -- stability 0-60.
  - Lower-left (low speed + small wing angle): deep green -- stability ~100.
  - Middle band: smooth green-to-red transition, ~640 points below stability 95.
  - DRS=0 and DRS=1 surfaces are near-identical (differ by < 1 point below 95).
- **Physical interpretation**: High speed with large wing angle generates high
  downforce but also triggers flow separation and turbulence -- the aeroelastic
  mechanism behind porpoising. The smooth gradient confirms the model has learned
  a physically plausible mapping rather than overfitting noise.
- **DRS invariance**: The negligible difference between DRS=0 and DRS=1 surfaces
  is consistent with the EDA finding (boxplot) that DRS has a limited direct
  effect on stability_index. DRS affects drag/downforce but stability is
  dominated by the speed-wing interaction.

## Key decision: model selection for PSO

- **Primary choice for PSO inference**: M3_XGBoost (R2=0.9999, 0.24 ms per call)
- **Uncertainty backup**: M6_DeepEnsemble (R2=0.9995, sigma available, 1.23 ms)
- **Accuracy backup**: M2_RandomForest (R2=1.000, but 37 ms per call -- too slow for PSO)
- **Rejected**: M1_Ridge (R2=0.633, below threshold), M5_TabNet (training 763 s, infer 7.85 ms, not competitive)
